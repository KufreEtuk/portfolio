About me

A bit about who I am and what I work with.

I'm Kufre, a final year Computer Science student at Pan-Atlantic University in Lagos. I got into programming because I wanted to understand how the apps I use every day actually work, and these days I mostly build web apps from the database up to the page.

On the back end I like Python with FastAPI, and I've been getting more into TypeScript for anything that runs on Node. I did my internship at MTN Nigeria, which gave me a feel for how real teams ship software. Right now I'm looking for internship and junior developer roles where I can keep learning.
